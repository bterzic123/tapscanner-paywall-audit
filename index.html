What this is

We opened TapScanner the way a new user does, then compared your paywall with the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market rather than inside one app. We installed TapScanner, went through to the paywall, closed it, read your App Store listing, and checked what is there against State of Paywalls 2026.

Benchmarks use all categories, because the report has no Business category. Your largest iOS region is Europe, but most of TapScanner’s revenue comes from Google Play and the report covers the App Store only. So we compare how your paywall is set up, and every number we quote describes the market, not your app.

The prototype rebuilds only your paywall and what happens when someone closes it. It uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your paywall

Every element in the prototype is yours: the photo, the headline, the feature carousel, the reminder toggle, the plan cards, the blue Continue button. Your 130M+ line and the Skystone review come from your own onboarding.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.
